Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. BillX runs in Chrome or Safari on any phone or computer. On a phone you can add it to the home screen like an app.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored in Google Cloud (Firebase), encrypted in transit and at rest, and only people you add can see it. You can download a full backup any time.

What happens after the free trial?

Choose a plan and pay by UPI Autopay, card or net banking. If you don't subscribe, your account becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and you can still view and download your bills.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from Settings → Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Can you set it up for us?

On the Pro plan we set up your logo, designs and item list, move data from your old software and train your staff.

Does it work for hospitals and clinics?

Yes. It has patient fields (UHID, IP/OP no., consultant, ward, admission and discharge dates), services grouped by department, and an insurance / TPA share with the balance the patient pays.
