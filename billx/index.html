Frequently asked questions

Is BillX free to try?

Yes. Start free on any phone or computer, no sign-up needed: make GST invoices, quotes and receipts, print or download them as PDF.

Do I need to install anything?

No. BillX runs in Chrome or Safari on any phone or computer. On a phone you can add it to the home screen like an app.

Is BillX GST compliant?

Yes. It shows GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes, place of supply, CGST + SGST or IGST (picked automatically from the state), a GST summary and the amount in words, and gives you GST reports by rate for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B.

How do I pay for BillX?

Pay BillX Solutions by UPI, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or bank transfer, then send the payment reference. Your plan is switched on after we confirm the payment. Prices start at ₹499 a month.

Is my data safe?

Your bills are saved on your own device and never uploaded. Download a full backup from Settings any time and restore it on another device.

Can you set it up for us?

On the Pro plan we set up your logo, designs and item list, move data from your old software and train your staff.

Does it work for hospitals and clinics?

Yes. It has patient fields (UHID, IP/OP no., consultant, ward, admission and discharge dates), services grouped by department, and an insurance / TPA share with the balance the patient pays.
