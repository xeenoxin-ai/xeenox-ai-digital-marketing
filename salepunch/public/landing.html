Questions

Good questions, straight answers

How does SalePunch record calls made from an agent's own phone?

Agents install the SalePunch Android app and tap Call on a lead. The app dials from the SIM in their phone, and the phone's own call recorder (built into most Samsung, Xiaomi, Vivo, OPPO, Realme and OnePlus phones, and the Google Phone app) records both voices. When the call ends, SalePunch reads the call length and uploads the recording automatically. Prefer a company cloud line? SalePunch also works with Exotel.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil and mixed speech. The summaries and coaching are always written in simple English for managers.

What does "call minutes analysed" mean?

The total length of the calls SalePunch transcribes and scores each month. A team making 40 five-minute calls a day uses about 4,000 minutes a month.

Do I need to buy new phones or SIMs?

No. Agents keep their own phones and company SIMs. They install the SalePunch app once and switch on automatic call recording in the phone's dialer. We walk you through it.

Is recording calls legal?

Yes, as long as you tell people. Tell leads the call is recorded (many phones announce it automatically). Recordings stay in your own account, in line with the DPDP Act 2023.

Can I connect my existing CRM?

Yes. New leads can come in from website forms, Meta Lead Ads, Zoho or HubSpot, and every call result can be sent back to your CRM automatically.
