EasyWay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026 (shared community data)

EasyWay is a free navigation app made by Xeenox AI. It has no accounts, no ads and no advertising or analytics tracking.

Location

With your permission, EasyWay uses your phone's location to start routes from where you are, show traffic hotspots around you and find nearby workshops, fuel and EV charging when your vehicle breaks down. EasyWay does not store your location on any server and does not keep a location history.

Map and route services

To draw the map and work out routes, EasyWay sends the places you search for and the start and end points of a route to free OpenStreetMap services: OpenStreetMap map tiles, Nominatim and Photon (place search), Valhalla and OSRM (routing) and Overpass (nearby places and traffic-causing features). These services receive the request and your IP address, like any website you visit, and are governed by their own privacy policies. EasyWay does not send them your name or any account details.

Shared community data (Google Firebase)

To help every driver, EasyWay keeps a small shared database on Google Firebase (Firestore). The app signs in anonymously: you get a random app ID, not linked to your name, phone number or Google account. Stored there:

  • Traffic jam reports you make: the map position, time and your random app ID. Shown to other drivers for one hour.
  • Anonymous traffic speeds while you navigate: roughly every 150 m, the area (about 150 m wide), time and your speed, without any app ID. Used to show slow roads to everyone. You can turn this off in Settings → "Share anonymous traffic speeds".
  • Ratings and reviews you post: the stars, your text, the place and your random app ID. Reviews are public. You can change your review at any time.
  • Places you add: what you type and the map position, with your random app ID. They are public.

Google processes this data for us under the Firebase terms. We do not sell or share it with anyone else. To have your data removed, open an issue at the contact link below.

Data kept on your phone

Your settings and the traffic jams you report are saved only on your phone. Jam reports are deleted automatically after one hour. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

AI assistant

The assistant runs on your phone. If you turn on the optional Ollama setting, your messages go only to the Ollama server address you enter yourself (normally your own computer).

Navigation and voice

Turn-by-turn navigation runs inside EasyWay. While you drive, your position is used on your phone to follow the route, and the start of a new route search is sent to the routing services above to find an easier way. Spoken directions use your phone's own text-to-speech.

Adding a missing place

If you use "Add a missing place", the name, type, phone and details you type and the map position are sent to OpenStreetMap as a public note, so volunteer mappers can add the place. Do not include personal information you don't want to be public. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

Phone calls

Tapping Call opens your phone's dialler.

Children

EasyWay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
