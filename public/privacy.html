TMap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

TMap is a free navigation app made by Xeenox AI. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Location

With your permission, TMap uses your phone's location to start routes from where you are, show traffic hotspots around you and find nearby workshops, fuel and EV charging when your vehicle breaks down. TMap does not store your location on any server and does not keep a location history.

Map and route services

To draw the map and work out routes, TMap sends the places you search for and the start and end points of a route to free OpenStreetMap services: OpenStreetMap map tiles, Nominatim and Photon (place search), Valhalla and OSRM (routing) and Overpass (nearby places and traffic-causing features). These services receive the request and your IP address, like any website you visit, and are governed by their own privacy policies. TMap does not send them your name or any account details.

Data kept on your phone

Your settings and the traffic jams you report are saved only on your phone. Jam reports are deleted automatically after one hour. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

AI assistant

The assistant runs on your phone. If you turn on the optional Ollama setting, your messages go only to the Ollama server address you enter yourself (normally your own computer).

Phone calls and Google Maps

Tapping Call opens your phone's dialler. Tapping Start opens the chosen route in Google Maps, where Google's privacy policy applies.

Children

TMap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
