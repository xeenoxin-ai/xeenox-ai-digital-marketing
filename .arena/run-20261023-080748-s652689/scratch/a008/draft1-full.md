# Xeenox → Bangalore: organic lead plan (GBP, social, AI answers) — DRAFT 1

## Assumptions
- Xeenox is based in Palakkad; no Bangalore office/GBP is known.
- Small team, founder Sudarshan, no paid budget in scope.
- Website exists but URL unknown; I write "xeenox.in" placeholders → replace.
- A lead = inbound call, WhatsApp, form or DM from a business owner in Bangalore asking about services.

## Gate 0 — Where is Xeenox physically?
Google's rule: a GBP needs a location where staff meet customers in person during listed hours, OR a service-area business based somewhere and serving nearby (Google guidance: ~2 hours' drive). Palakkad→Bangalore is ~6–7 hours. So a Palakkad-based SAB cannot honestly serve Bangalore and will be suspended on review.
Path A: staffed Bangalore office (coworking OK if a Xeenox person is there in stated hours, signage, own phone) → create listing.
Path B: no Bangalore presence → do NOT create a Bangalore GBP. You can still rank organic + AI + social. Decide in week 1.

## 4. AI Overviews / answer engines
How they pick: entity clarity + third-party corroboration + pages that answer the question directly.
Entity: identical name/description/sameAs; Organization schema; LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Wikidata? (Wikidata notability — skip or caution).
Corroboration: Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART (no), Bing Places (ChatGPT uses Bing), Apple Business Connect.
Content: answer-first pages; FAQ; comparison page honest; pricing page (LLMs cite pricing transparency); "AI-powered marketing agency Bangalore" differentiator.
Reddit/Quora: answer real questions as founder; no spam.
Technical: robots.txt allow GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended; no nosnippet; Google-Extended doesn't control AI Overviews.
Measurement: fixed 10 prompts monthly across Google AI Mode/AIO, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini; log.

## 5. Leads & measurement
Definition; tracking: GBP Performance, GA4 events (tel click, WhatsApp click, form), Search Console, LinkedIn; a "How did you hear" field; weekly sheet.
Tripwires: no reviews in 30 days; GBP suspended; page not indexed in 14 days; zero AI mentions after 90 days; leads not Bangalore.

## 6. Risks
Suspension; ASCI superlative; fake reviews; doorway pages; AI-generated thin content; "first fully AI-powered" claim unverifiable; "best" in name.
