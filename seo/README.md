## Read this first: what can and can't be done

1. **Map rankings depend on distance.** Google ranks map results by relevance, distance and prominence. A profile at HSR Layout can realistically reach the top 3 for searches made in HSR Layout, Bellandur, Koramangala, BTM, Agara and Sarjapur Road. It will **not** be #1 for someone searching from Yelahanka or Whitefield. Nobody can promise #1 across "all of Bangalore". To cover the whole city, use the website (organic results) and area pages, not the map pin.
2. **The GBP name must be your real business name.** Use exactly what is on your signage, invoice and website, for example `Xeenox Technologies`. Do not add "Best Digital Marketing Agency Bangalore" to the name. That is the most common reason profiles get suspended, and competitors report it.
3. **The address must be a real office where staff work and there is signage.** If 9th Main, Sector 7, HSR Layout is a virtual office or a coworking hot‑desk, Google can suspend the profile on re‑verification. If you have no staffed office, hide the address and set a service area instead.
4. **Reviews must be real.** Bought, swapped or incentivised reviews get filtered or get the listing penalised. Section 6 of the GBP file has a compliant process that still collects 10–15 reviews a month.
5. **I can't log into your GBP or create accounts on directories from here.** Everything in this folder is ready to paste. Run it in the order below.

## Execution order (90 days)

### Week 1 — fix the foundation (this matters most)
- [ ] Decide on **one** name and use it everywhere (GBP, website schema, footer, Facebook, Instagram, directories). Right now the site schema says `Xeenox` and the GBP says `Xeenox Technologies`.
- [ ] Fix the site issues in `website-fixes.md`: unique titles per page, a real H1, removing `/login` from the sitemap, and adding Bangalore case studies next to the Palakkad ones.
- [ ] GBP: set the primary category, secondary categories, description, services, hours and attributes from `gbp-optimization.md`.
- [ ] Upload 25+ real photos: office exterior with signage, interior, team, and screenshots of client results.
- [ ] Seed 10 Q&As.
- [ ] Replace the site JSON‑LD with `schema-localbusiness.json`, after filling in hours and coordinates.
- [ ] Create the GBP short review link and send the request to every past and current client (aim for 15+ reviews in the first two weeks).

### Weeks 2–4 — citations and reviews
- [ ] Submit the 20 Tier‑1 citations in `off-page-backlinks.md` using the exact NAP block.
- [ ] Post on GBP twice a week (posts are in `gbp-optimization.md`).
- [ ] Reply to every review within 24 hours, using the service and area naturally.
- [ ] Publish the HSR Layout and Koramangala area pages on the website.

### Days 30–60 — authority
- [ ] Tier‑2 citations and agency directories (Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush and similar). Ask clients to leave reviews there too.
- [ ] 4 guest posts and 2 PR mentions (see the backlink plan).
- [ ] Join and get listed on the local business association, startup and community pages.
- [ ] Publish 2 Bangalore case studies on the site.

### Days 60–90 — expand
- [ ] Add area pages for Bellandur, BTM, Sarjapur Road, Electronic City and Whitefield.
- [ ] Industry pages, for example real estate, clinics and restaurants in Bangalore.
- [ ] Keep posting, collecting reviews and building links each month.
