## 4. Content‑led backlinks (ongoing, 4–6 good links a month)

1. **Guest posts** on Indian marketing and business blogs, such as SocialSamosa, Afaqs, exchange4media, MarTech Advisor, Entrepreneur India, YourStory (contributor) and Inc42 (expert column). Pitch topics:
   - "How Bangalore clinics cut cost per lead by 40% with AI chatbots"
   - "Google Maps ranking factors for Indian local businesses in 2026"
   - "Meta Ads benchmarks for Bangalore real estate (our data)"
2. **Expert quotes (digital PR):** sign up on Qwoted, Featured.com, SourceBottle and Help a B2B Writer, and answer 3 queries a week on marketing and AI topics. Each published quote is a high‑authority link.
3. **Data study:** publish one "Bangalore Digital Marketing Benchmarks 2026" report with CPL and CTR by industry from your campaigns (anonymised). Then pitch it to the media listed above. One good data piece earns links for years.
4. **Free tools:** for example a "Google Ads Cost per Lead Calculator — India" or a "GBP Audit Checklist" PDF. Tools pick up links naturally.
5. **Podcasts and YouTube collaborations** with Bangalore founders. Show notes link back.
6. **Quora, Reddit (r/bangalore, r/IndianEntrepreneur, r/digital_marketing) and LinkedIn:** give useful answers and only link when it genuinely helps. This drives brand searches and referral traffic more than direct ranking.
7. **Broken‑link building:** find dead links on "digital marketing resources" pages of Indian blogs (Ahrefs → Broken links) and offer your article as a replacement.
8. **Unlinked mentions:** search Google for `"Xeenox" -site:xeenox.in` every month and ask anyone who mentions you without a link to add one.

## 6. Avoid (these get sites penalised)
- ❌ Fiverr "1000 backlinks" or "DA 50+ PBN" packages
- ❌ Paid "guest posts" on sites that exist only to sell posts (check: real traffic? Real authors?)
- ❌ Fake reviews, review exchanges, or paying for reviews on Google, Justdial or Clutch
- ❌ Copy‑pasted descriptions with keyword‑stuffed business names on directories
- ❌ Creating a second GBP or a fake address to "cover more areas"
- ❌ Link exchanges with unrelated sites ("you link me, I link you")
