## 6. Reviews (the biggest gap versus competitors)

### Review link
GBP → **Ask for reviews** → copy the `g.page/r/...` link. Turn it into a QR code and put it on your invoice, email signature and reception desk.

### When to ask
- When a client reports a good result (a lead spike or a good monthly report).
- 7 days after a website goes live.
- Ask every past client once now. **Never** offer discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews, and do not ask only happy clients (that is "review gating", which is against policy).

### WhatsApp message (send to clients)
> Hi {Name}, thanks for working with Xeenox Technologies! 🙏 If you're happy with the {SEO / ads / website} work, could you leave us a quick Google review? It really helps other Bangalore businesses find us.
> 👉 {review link}
> It takes 30 seconds. If you can, mention the service we did and your area. Thank you!

### Email version
**Subject:** Quick favour, {Name}?
> Hi {Name},
> It's been great helping {Company} with {service}. If you have a minute, would you share your experience on Google? Honest feedback helps us improve and helps other businesses decide.
> {review link}
> Thanks,
> {Your name}, Xeenox Technologies

### Reply templates (reply to 100% of reviews within 24 hours)
- **5★:** "Thank you, {Name}! It was a pleasure running {Google Ads / SEO} for {Company} — great to see the leads coming in from {area}. Looking forward to scaling it further. — Team Xeenox Technologies, HSR Layout"
- **4★:** "Thanks for the feedback, {Name}! Glad the {service} is working. We'd love to know what would make it 5★ — we'll call you this week."
- **1–3★:** "Hi {Name}, we're sorry this didn't meet expectations. We take this seriously — please call us on +91 90745 80473 so we can understand and fix it." (Stay calm, do not argue, and take the conversation offline.)

Target: 15 reviews in the first 2 weeks, then a steady 10+ every month. Steady numbers look natural; a sudden burst gets filtered.
