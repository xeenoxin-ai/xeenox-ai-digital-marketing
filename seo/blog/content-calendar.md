## Notes

- **Publishing order** within a month can swap if a seasonal topic suits it.
- **Spin-offs**: each GBP post links back to the article and uses one real photo. Do not post stock images as if they were your work.
- **Case studies**: wherever a post says "example", use a placeholder until a client approves figures. Real clients include Skypool Fitness Studio, Muddu Magu Hospital, Dr Heal Pain Cure Hospital, SB Felines, Trioz Steel Windows & Doors and Universal Electricals & Plumbing.
- **Refresh**: review posts 1 to 3 every six months for pricing and tool changes.
- **Measure**: track organic clicks per post in Search Console, and calls, form fills and WhatsApp clicks from the post pages. Content usually takes 2-4 months to show movement.
