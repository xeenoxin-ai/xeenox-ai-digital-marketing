## What makes Koramangala different

Marketing in Koramangala is not the same as in HSR, even though the two are neighbours.

- **Startup and D2C base.** Koramangala has long been a hub for startups, and many founders work from offices and coworking spaces here. Brands selling online often use Koramangala as their base even if customers are across India. That means two audiences: local walk-ins and national buyers.
- **Food and beverage depth.** The 80 Feet Road, 5th Block and the area around Forum Mall and the Sony World junction are known for restaurants, cafes, bars and dessert brands. Discovery here runs through Google Maps, Instagram and delivery apps, and reviews and photos decide the winner.
- **Many blocks, many micro-markets.** Koramangala is divided into numbered blocks (1st to 8th and beyond). People search by block or landmark, not by the area name alone, which changes which keywords and which map pins matter.
- **Higher competition for every ad click.** More advertisers per search means costs per click tend to be higher than in quieter neighbourhoods. Tracking cost per lead, not clicks, is essential.
