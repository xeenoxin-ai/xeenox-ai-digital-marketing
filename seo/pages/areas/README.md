# Area Pages: Template and Rules

Area pages target "digital marketing agency in [locality]". Done well, they help us show up for searches in nearby localities. Done badly (same text with the name swapped), Google treats them as doorway pages. This file sets the rules.

Live: HSR Layout, Koramangala. Next: Bellandur, BTM Layout, Sarjapur Road, Electronic City, Whitefield, Indiranagar.

## Rules

1. **Build a page only if we can write at least 60% unique, locality-specific content.** If we cannot, do not publish it.
2. **Never claim an office we do not have.** Our only address is 9th Main Road, Sector 7, HSR Layout. Area pages are service-area pages. No fake addresses, no virtual-office pins, no extra GBP listings.
3. **No fabricated clients, results or reviews.** Use placeholders until approved. Only name real clients with permission.
4. **Do not copy text between area pages.** Run a similarity check against the other area pages before publishing. If paragraphs overlap, rewrite.
5. **Distances and travel times must be checked on Google Maps** before publishing, at off-peak and peak.
6. **One primary keyword per page**: "digital marketing agency in [locality]". Do not add a second locality.
7. **GBP name stays "Xeenox Technologies".** No locality keywords in the name. Category: Internet marketing service.
8. **Unique proof per page**: at least one locality-specific element such as a case study, local business mix, landmark guide, or a data point from our own GBP Insights or Search Console.
9. **Link both ways**: each page links to 2 relevant services, 1 relevant blog post and 2 nearby area pages. The homepage and footer link to every live area page.
10. **Honest proximity note**: say plainly that Maps rankings depend on distance from the searcher.

## Template (copy and fill)

```
# Area page: Digital Marketing Agency in [Locality]
URL: /areas/digital-marketing-agency-[locality-slug]
Primary keyword: digital marketing agency in [Locality]

Title (under 60): Digital Marketing Agency in [Locality], Bangalore | Xeenox
Meta (under 160): [Unique angle in one line] + [service] + CTA
H1: Digital Marketing Agency in [Locality] / or an angle-specific H1

1. Intro (60-80 words): who we help here, where our office is relative to them
2. What makes [Locality] different (150-200 words): LOCAL specifics only
3. Local business mix and table of needs (100-150 words)
4. How we serve this locality (150-200 words): tied to the mix, not generic
5. Results / case study placeholders
6. Distance and directions from HSR Layout office (50-100 words) + embedded map note
7. FAQ (4-5 local questions, 120-200 words)
8. CTA with NAP
9. Internal links (2 services, 1 blog, 2 nearby areas)
10. Schema: Service with areaServed + provider @id https://xeenox.in/#business; FAQPage if FAQ visible
Target length: 700-1,000 words
```

## What does not belong on area pages

- Lists of 30 localities in one page
- Copy-pasted paragraphs with the locality swapped
- "Best digital marketing agency in X" without proof
- Fake reviews, fake addresses, fake case studies
- Keyword-stuffed footers
