## The problem: a good-looking site that doesn't bring business

We often see sites that look fine on a designer's laptop and fail on a customer's phone.

- **Slow on mobile.** Heavy images and bulky templates make pages crawl on a 4G connection in Whitefield or a basement clinic.
- **No obvious next step.** Phone number hidden in the footer, no WhatsApp button, a contact form with eleven fields.
- **Built for looks, not search.** One long home page, no pages for individual services or areas, so Google has nothing to show.
- **Unclear who it's for.** "Welcome to our company" instead of "Knee pain treatment in HSR Layout".
- **Can't be updated.** Every small change needs a call to the developer and a wait of a week.

## Questions we get asked

### How long does it take to build a website?

A small business site is usually a matter of weeks, and larger sites take longer. We give you a date in writing before we start, based on how quickly you can send content and feedback.

### Will my website show up on Google?

A well-built site gives you a good start with clean structure, fast loading and proper titles. Ranking for competitive searches also needs ongoing SEO, which we offer as a separate service.

### Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes, for everyday changes like text, photos and offers. We pick the platform based on who will manage the site and how often it changes, and we train your team after launch.

### Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We check what works now, keep the pages that already rank and redirect old addresses properly, so you do not lose the traffic you have.

### Do I own the website and domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name and you receive access to everything at handover.

### Can you add a WhatsApp chatbot or booking system?

Yes. We can connect a chatbot that answers common questions and collects details, and add booking or payment features where your business needs them. Pricing depends on the scope.
