## The problem: your site gets visits but the phone stays quiet

Search in Bangalore is crowded. A dentist in Indiranagar, a builder on Sarjapur Road and a skincare brand all compete with aggregators and national names for the same first page. Most small businesses lose in three ways.

- **Wrong keywords.** Ranking for your own name, or for broad terms nobody buys from, brings visitors but not customers.
- **A slow, thin website.** Most searches here happen on a phone, often on patchy data. A page that needs six seconds to open loses the visitor before it loads.
- **Mixed-up local signals.** Your Google listing, website and directory entries show slightly different names, numbers or addresses. Google trusts none of them fully.

Then the monthly report arrives with impressions and keyword positions, and nobody can say how many calls came from search.

## Questions we get asked

### How long does SEO take to show results in Bangalore?

Most sites see early movement in two to three months and steadier enquiries from month four to six, depending on competition and the state of your website. Anyone promising page one in 30 days for a competitive search is guessing.

### Do you guarantee the number one spot on Google?

No, and no honest agency can. Google decides rankings. We commit to a clear plan, work delivered on time, and reports that show calls, form fills and WhatsApp clicks, not only keyword positions.

### How is SEO different from Google Ads?

Google Ads brings visitors as long as you pay for clicks. SEO takes longer, but the traffic keeps coming when the budget stops. Many Bangalore businesses run ads for quick enquiries and SEO for steady ones.

### How do you use AI in SEO?

AI speeds up keyword clustering, content briefs, competitor checks and reporting. A person reviews every page before it is published, so the writing sounds like your business and the facts are right.

### Do I need a new website to do SEO?

Not always. We audit first. If your site can be fixed, we fix it. If it is built in a way that blocks ranking or loads too slowly, we tell you plainly and quote a rebuild separately.
