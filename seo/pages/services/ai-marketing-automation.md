## The problem: you pay for leads, then lose them

A typical week for a growing Bangalore business: ad leads land in email, Google Sheets, WhatsApp and a notebook. Someone calls a few of them. The rest wait.

- **No single place for leads.** Nobody knows who enquired, who was called and who said "call me next week".
- **Follow-up depends on memory.** It works while the owner has time and fails during a busy week.
- **Customers forgotten after the sale.** No reminder for a repeat visit, a renewal or a review.
- **Reports built by hand.** Someone spends the first days of each month copying numbers from Google, Meta and Sheets.
- **Tools that don't talk to each other.** The data exists, but in five different places.

## What our marketing automation agency in Bangalore automates

- **Lead capture:** forms, ads, website and WhatsApp into one CRM or sheet, with duplicate checks
- **Instant response:** WhatsApp or email acknowledgement within seconds of an enquiry
- **Lead scoring and routing:** serious leads go to the right salesperson, with a task and a deadline
- **Follow-up sequences:** timed messages across WhatsApp, email and SMS for leads who go quiet
- **Appointment and visit reminders:** fewer no-shows for clinics, studios and site visits
- **Review requests:** a message after each visit, with your Google review link
- **Repeat and renewal campaigns:** membership expiry, annual check-ups, reorders
- **Missed-call follow-up:** a message sent to every caller you could not answer
- **Reporting:** one dashboard that pulls ad spend, leads and bookings, with a weekly summary sent to you

**Where AI helps:** scoring leads from their answers, drafting message variations, summarising chats for your team and writing the plain-language report. The rules and approvals stay with your team.

## How an automation project runs

1. **Map the journey.** From first click to repeat customer, we draw what happens today, step by step.
2. **Find the leaks.** Where leads wait, drop or get forgotten. We pick the two or three fixes that pay back fastest.
3. **Choose the tools.** We use what you already have where possible and add only what is needed.
4. **Build and connect.** Flows are built and tested with your own sample leads.
5. **Train your team.** A short session on what the system does and what people still need to do.
6. **Watch and improve.** In the first weeks, we check the flows daily and fix anything that misfires.

## Questions we get asked

### Do I need expensive software for marketing automation?

Not usually. We start with tools you already use, such as Google Sheets, WhatsApp and a simple CRM. We recommend paid tools only when the saving clearly beats the cost.

### Will automation make my business feel impersonal?

Not if it is done well. Automation handles the quick, repeated steps like acknowledgements, reminders and follow-ups, so your team has more time for real conversations.

### What can be automated on WhatsApp?

Instant replies, appointment reminders, follow-up sequences, review requests and chatbot conversations. WhatsApp has rules on business messaging, such as approved templates and customer opt-in, and we build within them.

### How is this different from a chatbot?

A chatbot handles the live conversation. Automation covers everything around it: routing leads, scheduling follow-ups, updating your CRM and sending reports. They work best together.

### How long does it take to see the benefit?

Simple flows like instant replies and reminders can go live within a couple of weeks, and the effect on follow-up speed is visible straight away. Bigger changes to your sales process take longer.

### Is my customers' data safe?

We use accounts in your name, give access only to the people who need it and avoid copying data into places it does not need to be. We explain where your data sits before we build.
