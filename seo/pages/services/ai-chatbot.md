## The problem: enquiries go cold while you're busy

In Bangalore, people message three businesses and go with the first one that replies. Your team, meanwhile, is with a patient, on a site visit or asleep.

- **Slow replies.** A lead who waits hours is usually gone by the time you answer.
- **Same questions, all day.** Timings, fees, location, "is it available?" take up hours your staff could spend on selling.
- **Time-wasters mixed with buyers.** Without a few simple questions up front, your best people spend time on enquiries that were never serious.
- **Nobody follows up.** If a lead says "I'll think about it", most businesses never message again.
- **Ad leads sitting in a spreadsheet.** You paid for them, and they are not being called.

## Questions we get asked

### What is the difference between a WhatsApp chatbot and the WhatsApp Business app?

The Business app is for a person to reply manually, with a few automatic messages. A chatbot uses the WhatsApp Business Platform to answer, ask questions, book and follow up automatically, and it can handle many chats at once.

### Will customers know they are talking to a bot?

We tell them clearly at the start, and always offer a way to reach a person. Customers are fine with a bot when it replies quickly and helps.

### Can the chatbot take bookings and payments?

It can take bookings and share payment links. We connect it to your calendar or sheet for bookings, and to your payment provider where needed.

### Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?

Often yes, but the number must be set up on the WhatsApp Business Platform, which changes how it works in the normal app. We check this with you before moving anything.

### What if the bot gets a question it can't answer?

It says so politely and hands the chat to your team, with the conversation attached. We add the missing answer afterwards so it is handled next time.

### How long does a chatbot take to set up?

A simple lead and FAQ bot can be ready within a couple of weeks. Bookings, CRM links or several languages take longer. We give a date once we understand your flow.
