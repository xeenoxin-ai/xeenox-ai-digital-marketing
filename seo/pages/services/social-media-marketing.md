## The problem: you post, but nothing happens

Most small businesses in Bangalore already post. The effort is real, the results are not.

- **No plan.** Posts appear when someone has time, so the page goes quiet for weeks, then floods.
- **Content about you, not for them.** Festival greetings and stock photos get a few likes from people who were never going to buy.
- **Reels that don't stop the scroll.** The first three seconds decide everything, and most start with a logo.
- **Unanswered comments and DMs.** The most interested people, the ones who ask "price?", are left waiting.
- **No link to the business.** Nobody can say which post brought an enquiry.

## What our social media marketing agency in Bangalore manages for you

- A monthly content calendar built around what your customers ask, doubt and want to see
- Reels, carousels, stories and posters, shot and edited for mobile
- Captions in the language your audience uses, including English with Kannada or Hindi where it fits
- Posting at the times your followers are active
- Comment and DM replies, with serious enquiries moved to WhatsApp
- Profile set-up: bio, highlights, link-in-bio and a clear "what to do next"
- Community building: collaborations, local tags and user-generated content
- Paid boosts for posts that are already working
- A monthly report on reach that matters: profile visits, saves, DMs and enquiries

**Where AI helps:** idea generation, caption drafts, trying more hooks for each reel and tidying up reporting. Everything is edited by a person for tone and accuracy before it goes out.

## How we work with you

1. **Listen.** A call about your customers, your busiest days, your best-selling services and what you want people to do.
2. **Look around.** We review your pages and 5 to 10 local competitors to find gaps.
3. **Plan the month.** You approve a calendar with themes, formats and offers before we create anything.
4. **Create and post.** We shoot or edit, schedule and publish. You send us raw clips and photos, or we visit for a shoot.
5. **Reply and nurture.** We handle comments and DMs inside agreed hours and pass real enquiries to you.
6. **Review.** Every month we look at what worked, drop what did not and plan the next batch.

## Questions we get asked

### How many posts and reels will you make each month?

It depends on your plan and goals. A typical monthly plan includes a mix of reels, carousels and static posts, and we agree the exact number in writing before we start.

### Do I need to provide photos and videos?

It helps if you send raw clips from your phone, and we edit them. If you would rather not, we can arrange a shoot at your location for an agreed fee.

### Will you reply to comments and messages?

Yes, within agreed hours. Simple questions get answered by us. Enquiries that are ready to buy go to you on WhatsApp, or to our chatbot if you use one.

### Can social media really bring customers, or only followers?

It can bring customers when posts are tied to a clear offer and enquiries are followed up quickly. We track DMs, calls and WhatsApp messages from your pages, not only likes.

### Do you manage ads on Instagram and Facebook too?

Yes, as a separate service. Many clients start with organic content, then add ads once the page is in good shape. We can combine both.

### How do you use AI in your content work?

We use AI to brainstorm ideas, draft captions, try different hooks and speed up reporting. Every post is reviewed by our team so it sounds like your brand and the facts are right.
