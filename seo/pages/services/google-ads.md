## The problem: clicks are expensive and half of them are wasted

Bangalore is one of the costlier cities for clicks. Real estate, healthcare and education searches can be pricey, and a few badly chosen keywords can burn a month's budget in days. The usual leaks:

- **Broad keywords with no negatives.** You pay when someone searching for a job, a free option or a different service clicks your ad.
- **Ads that lead to the home page.** The visitor has to hunt for what they came for, and leaves.
- **Conversions not tracked properly.** Google cannot learn which clicks bring buyers if calls and WhatsApp taps are not counted.
- **Nobody checks the search terms.** The same wasted clicks repeat every week.
- **Slow follow-up.** An enquiry that waits a day for a callback is half lost already.

## How a campaign runs

1. **Goal and numbers.** What is an enquiry worth to you? What can you pay for one? We start here.
2. **Research and structure.** Keywords, competitors, locations and a budget split.
3. **Build and track.** Campaigns, ads, landing pages and conversion tracking go live together, not one after another.
4. **Learn for two to four weeks.** We watch search terms and cut what does not work before spending scales.
5. **Scale what works.** Budgets go to the campaigns with the lowest cost per enquiry.
6. **Report weekly and monthly.** Spend, enquiries, cost per enquiry and what we are changing next.

### Who owns the ad account?

You do. The account is in your name and you can see all spend and results at any time. If we part ways, you keep everything.

### How soon will I get enquiries?

Search ads can bring enquiries from the first week. The first two to four weeks are for learning what works, so cost per enquiry usually falls after that.

### Do you track phone calls and WhatsApp enquiries, or only form fills?

Google catches people already searching for what you sell. Meta finds people who might want it. Searches like "emergency plumber" suit Google, while an offer like a new studio opening can suit Meta. We tell you honestly which to start with.
