## The problem: lots of leads, few customers

Meta can produce leads at a low cost per form, which is exactly the trap. Many Bangalore advertisers see this pattern:

- **Leads who don't remember enquiring.** One-tap forms get filled by accident or curiosity.
- **Creative that tires quickly.** The same image or reel runs for weeks until people scroll past.
- **Slow follow-up.** A lead contacted after six hours is competing with three other businesses that replied in six minutes.
- **Targeting too wide or too narrow.** Either you reach everyone, or the audience is so small that costs climb.
- **Reports full of reach and clicks**, with no answer to "how many came in, and what did each cost?"

## From first ad to steady leads

1. **Offer first.** A strong offer beats clever targeting. We refine yours, or help create one.
2. **Creative batch.** We make a set of ad variations with different hooks, so the data shows what people respond to.
3. **Test small.** A modest budget over the first week or two tells us which creative and audience to back.
4. **Qualify and follow up.** New leads get an instant WhatsApp reply with a few questions, so your team calls the serious ones first.
5. **Scale or stop.** Winners get more budget. Losers are switched off without sentiment.
6. **Report on outcomes.** Leads, qualified leads, bookings or sales, and what each cost.

## Questions we get asked

### How much ad budget do I need to start?

Enough for Meta to gather data, which depends on your offer and audience size. We suggest a testing budget after a short call, and we keep the first phase small until we know what works.

### How do you improve lead quality?

We add qualifying questions, use WhatsApp conversations instead of one-tap forms where it suits, and send conversion data back to Meta so it finds more people like your real customers.

### Do you make the ad creatives too?

Yes. We design the images and edit short videos, and we use AI to produce more variations to test. You approve everything before it goes live.

### Can you run ads for a clinic or hospital?

Yes, with care. Healthcare ads must avoid guaranteed results and misleading claims, and Meta has its own health advertising policies. We write to those rules.

### Who responds to the leads?

Either your team or our chatbot first. The chatbot replies in seconds, asks a few questions and passes serious leads to your staff. Speed of reply matters a lot for Meta leads.

### How soon will I see results?

Leads can start within days of launch. It usually takes two to four weeks to learn which creative and audience work best, after which cost per lead tends to settle.
