## The problem: the map shows three businesses, and you are not one

For searches like "physiotherapy near me" or "gym in Bellandur", Google shows a map with three listings before any website. Most of the calls go to those three. If you are fourth, you are close to invisible.

Bangalore makes this harder than a small town does.

- **Distance matters.** A profile that ranks well in HSR Layout can be missing in Whitefield, because Google weighs how close the searcher is.
- **Profiles are set up once and forgotten.** Wrong category, no services listed, three-year-old photos, reviews with no reply.
- **Duplicates and old details.** A previous address or a second listing splits your reputation in two.
- **Reviews are left to chance.** Happy customers rarely post unless someone asks them, and asks them easily.

## Questions we get asked

### How long does it take to rank on Google Maps?

Fixing basics like categories and duplicate listings can show movement in a few weeks. Strong, steady map positions for competitive searches usually take two to four months, helped by reviews and consistent details.

### Can you help if my profile is suspended or has duplicates?

Yes. We check what triggered the problem, correct the details that broke Google's rules and file the reinstatement or merge request. We cannot promise the outcome, as Google makes the final call.

### Do you buy or write fake reviews?

Never. Fake reviews can get a profile suspended and they damage trust. We help you collect real reviews from real customers, and write reply drafts for you to approve.

### I have two branches in Bangalore. Is that covered?

Yes. Each branch needs its own profile, details and page. We price multi-branch work after seeing how many locations and areas are involved.

### What if I don't have a shop, only a service area?

Google allows service-area profiles for businesses that travel to customers. We set yours up with the areas you cover, without displaying a home address.

### Is local SEO better than running ads?

They do different jobs. Ads give you enquiries from the day they start, while you pay per click. A strong map profile keeps bringing calls without that cost. Many local businesses use both.
