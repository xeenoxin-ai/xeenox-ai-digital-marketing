Many agencies say "AI". Here is what it means in our work:

- **Faster research and testing.** AI helps us analyse search results, competitors and ad data in hours instead of days, so more budget goes into testing ideas.

### 1. What does a digital marketing agency in Bangalore do?
It plans and runs the channels that bring customers online: SEO, Google Maps, Google and Meta Ads, social media, your website and follow-up automation. At Xeenox we start with the channel closest to revenue for your business, usually Google Business Profile and search for local services.

### 2. How much does digital marketing cost in Bangalore?
It depends on scope and ad budget. Agency fees and ad spend are separate; ad spend goes to Google or Meta. See our guide on [digital marketing cost in Bangalore](/blog/digital-marketing-cost-in-bangalore) for typical market ranges, or [ask for a quote](/contact).

### 3. How soon will I see results?
Paid ads can bring enquiries within days of launch. Google Maps changes usually show in weeks. SEO on your website takes months. We tell you which to expect for your situation before you sign.

### 4. What makes Xeenox "AI-powered"?
We use AI for research, testing, reporting and instant lead response through chatbots and automation. Strategy and creative decisions are made by our team.

### 5. Do you work with businesses outside HSR Layout?
Yes. We serve businesses across Bangalore and in Kerala. Meetings can be in person at our HSR Layout office or on video.

### 6. Do you guarantee a number-one ranking?
