# About Xeenox Technologies

Xeenox Technologies is a digital marketing agency in HSR Layout, Bengaluru. We help local-service businesses, clinics, studios and manufacturers get found on Google and Maps and turn that visibility into calls, WhatsApp chats and leads. We use AI to work faster and respond to leads sooner, and we report on enquiries rather than vanity numbers.

- **Real reviews only.** We help you ask real customers for reviews. We do not buy, swap or fake them.
