## 3. Area keywords — Priority A for nearby areas, B for others
Pattern: `digital marketing agency in {area}`, `seo company in {area}`, `{service} {area}`.

The HSR Layout cluster is closest to the GBP pin, so start there:
- digital marketing agency in hsr layout ⭐
- digital marketing company hsr layout
- seo company in hsr layout
- digital marketing agency koramangala ⭐
- digital marketing agency bellandur
- digital marketing agency btm layout
- digital marketing agency sarjapur road
- digital marketing agency agara / bommanahalli

Wider Bangalore (needs area pages and the website, since the map pin is too far away to rank there):
- digital marketing agency electronic city
- digital marketing agency whitefield
- digital marketing agency marathahalli
- digital marketing agency indiranagar
- digital marketing agency jp nagar / jayanagar
- digital marketing agency mg road
- digital marketing agency yelahanka / hebbal
- digital marketing agency rajajinagar / malleshwaram

Each area page needs **unique** content: clients served there, local landmarks, a map embed and area‑specific FAQs. If you copy the same 300 words and swap the area name, Google treats the pages as doorway pages and ignores them.
