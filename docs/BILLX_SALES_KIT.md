## 2. The 2-minute shop demo (on your phone)

1. **"How long does a bill take you now?"** Let them answer.
2. Open **Quick Bill**. Tap 3 items. *"That's the bill."* (5 seconds)
3. Tap **UPI**. *"Your customer scans this. The amount is already in it. Money goes straight to your bank. No commission."*
4. Tap **Payment received**. *"Next customer is ready. The bill can go to them on WhatsApp."*
5. Tap **Today**. *"At closing time you see today's total, cash vs UPI vs card, and your best sellers. No more counting confusion."*
6. Open **Dashboard → Get paid**. *"Anyone who owes you money is listed here. One tap sends a polite WhatsApp reminder."*
7. **Close:** *"Try it free for 7 days. I'll set it up for you right now, with your menu or items."*
   Then set it up on their phone, import their items, add their UPI ID, and make their first real bill.

**Golden rule:** never leave a shop without making their first bill in BillX.
