# Trioz Steel Doors and Windows: "A Strong First Impression"

**Format:** 30-second motion graphics video, vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920), 30 fps
**Use:** Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Status
**Story:** A plain, forgettable entrance turns into a stylish steel door in one dramatic reveal.
**Feeling:** Starts flat and grey, then becomes warm, confident and welcoming.

---

## 1. Visual style guide

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Look | Clean, premium, architectural. 3D or 2.5D renders with soft studio lighting and slow, steady camera moves. No clutter. |
| Palette, "before" | Washed-out greys and beige, low contrast, flat daylight |
| Palette, "after" | Deep charcoal `#1E1F22`, brushed steel silver `#B9BEC4`, warm amber accent `#E8A23A`, off-white text `#F5F3EE` |
| Typeface | One bold geometric sans for headlines (e.g. Montserrat ExtraBold or Poppins Bold) and the same family in Medium for supporting lines |
| Text size (at 1080 px wide) | Headlines 88–110 px, supporting lines 56–64 px. Never below 48 px. |
| Text rules | No more than 5 words per line and 2 lines per card. Keep each card on screen for at least 1.5 s. Off-white text on a dark semi-transparent plate (60–70% opacity) when it sits over busy imagery. |
| Safe zones | Keep all text and the logo inside the centre band: 250 px clear at the top, 400 px at the bottom, 80 px at each side, so platform UI never covers them. |
| Motion language | Ease-in-out curves, nothing bouncy. Text enters with a short upward slide plus fade (8–10 frames). Steel elements move with weight: slow start, firm stop. |
| Product shown | Use Trioz's real door and window designs (photos, CAD files or approved renders). Do not add hardware, finishes, locks or features that Trioz has not confirmed. |

## 2. Scene-by-scene timeline

### Scene 1: The ordinary entrance (0:00–0:03)
- **Visual:** Front-on view of a dull home entrance: a plain, faded door, bare wall, flat grey daylight. The camera holds almost still, with a very slow push-in.
- **On-screen text:** **Your entrance speaks first.**
- **Animation:** The text fades up line by line at 0:00.5. A subtle film grain and low saturation make the scene feel "ordinary".
- **Transition out:** Hard cut on a soft bass pulse.

### Scene 2: The problem (0:03–0:07)
- **Visual:** Three quick close-ups, each about 1.3 s: a dated door handle, a plain frame edge, a bland doorstep. Everything stays grey and lifeless.
- **On-screen text:** **What is it saying?**
- **Animation:** Each close-up slides up into frame (a vertical swipe that feels native to mobile). The text holds in the centre band throughout.
- **Transition out:** The image desaturates fully to grey, then a thin vertical line of warm light appears down the middle of the door.

### Scene 3: The build-up (0:07–0:10)
- **Visual:** Back to the full entrance. The light line glows brighter and the camera pushes in faster. The music drops to near-silence with a rising whoosh.
- **On-screen text:** **Time for a change.**
- **Animation:** The text appears, then cracks apart along the light line as the line widens.
- **Transition out:** At exactly **0:10.0** (the beat drop), a white-amber light burst fills the frame for 4 frames.

### Scene 4: The dramatic reveal (0:10–0:15)
- **Visual:** As the light clears, the old door splits along the seam and both halves slide outward and dissolve. In its place, the Trioz steel door builds itself: first a glowing silver wireframe outline, then solid steel panels lock into place piece by piece (the frame, then the leaf, then the handle last). The wall turns charcoal and warm amber lights switch on around the entrance.
- **On-screen text:** **Meet your new entrance.** (appears at 0:12.5, after the door is complete)
- **Animation:** Each panel lands on a beat with a small metallic "clank" sound and a light camera shake (2–3 px). A slow light sweep glides across the steel surface from left to right as it settles.
- **Transition out:** Match cut. The camera pushes into the door frame and continues straight into the first detail shot.

### Scene 5: Design details (0:15–0:20)
- **Visual:** Three slow macro glides, about 1.6 s each, all on the real product:
  1. The frame edge and its lines
  2. The handle with a light glint
  3. The surface pattern or finish of the panel
- **On-screen text** (one per shot):
  - **Bold lines.**
  - **Modern style.**
  - **Made to stand out.**
- **Animation:** Gentle parallax and shallow depth of field. Each text card slides up with the camera move and fades out before the next one arrives.
- **Transition out:** A light-sweep wipe from bottom to top.

### Scene 6: The inviting entrance (0:20–0:24)
- **Visual:** A wide hero shot of the finished entrance at golden hour: the steel door, matching Trioz windows on either side, warm interior light glowing, a plant by the step. The door swings open slowly toward the viewer, as if inviting them in.
- **On-screen text:** **An entrance that welcomes.**
- **Animation:** Slow dolly-in. Warm light spills from the opening door across the frame.
- **Transition out:** A vertical split line slides in from the left.

### Scene 7: Before and after (0:24–0:27)
- **Visual:** Split screen with "before" (grey, ordinary) on the left and "after" (charcoal, steel, warm light) on the right. The divider sweeps from left to right, fully revealing the "after" view.
- **On-screen text:** **Before.** (over left) / **After.** (over right)
- **Animation:** The labels sit in small pill-shaped plates. "Before" fades out as the divider passes it.
- **Transition out:** The "after" image blurs and darkens into the end card background.

### Scene 8: End card and invitation (0:27–0:30)
- **Visual:** Charcoal background with a soft steel texture. The Trioz logo sits in the centre and is drawn by a thin silver light trace.
- **On-screen text:**
  - **Make a strong first impression.**
  - **Trioz Steel Doors and Windows**
  - **Explore the range today**
  - *[Client to supply: website / phone / WhatsApp / showroom address. Leave this line blank until it is confirmed.]*
- **Animation:** The logo is drawn in 0:27.0–0:28.0. The headline slides up at 0:28.0 and the call to action at 0:28.5. A last amber light sweep crosses the logo at 0:29.3. Hold to 0:30.
- **Transition out:** Hold, no fade, so the end card can loop cleanly into Scene 1.

## 4. Music and sound

**Music direction:** A modern cinematic track that builds into a drop, about 100–120 BPM, with one clear hit at 0:10.
- **0:00–0:07:** Minimal, muted plucks or a soft, low synth pad. Slightly flat and unexciting on purpose.
- **0:07–0:10:** A rising riser or whoosh, then a half-second of near-silence.
- **0:10–0:24:** An impact hit, then a confident groove (deep bass, crisp percussion, a warm synth or piano motif).
- **0:24–0:30:** The track resolves into a clean final note on the logo.

**Search terms for royalty-free libraries** (Artlist, Epidemic Sound, Envato, YouTube Audio Library, or Instagram's licensed audio): *"cinematic corporate build drop"*, *"modern luxury reveal"*, *"minimal tech trailer"*, *"architectural premium ambient"*. Confirm the licence covers paid social ads if the video will be boosted.

**Sound effects:**
- Soft bass pulse (0:03)
- Riser and whoosh (0:07–0:10)
- Impact boom with a light burst (0:10)
- Metallic "clank" as each steel panel locks into place (0:10–0:12.5)
- Subtle shimmer on light sweeps (0:14, 0:20, 0:29.3)
- Gentle door-swing sound (0:21)

**Mix:** Keep music at about −14 LUFS overall. If using voiceover, duck the music by 6–8 dB under the voice.

## 6. Claims and content guardrails

The script deliberately contains **no** product specifications, materials grades, certifications, prices, offers, warranties or guarantees, security or weather-resistance claims, or contact details. Only visual and emotional language is used ("bold lines", "modern style", "made to stand out", "an entrance that welcomes").

Before final render, the client must supply or approve:
- [ ] Logo files (SVG or high-resolution PNG) and any brand colours or fonts that should replace the suggested palette
- [ ] Real product images, CAD files or approved renders of the door and window designs to feature
- [ ] The exact call-to-action details for the end card (website, phone, WhatsApp, showroom); leave the line blank until these are confirmed
- [ ] Sign-off that every design shown in the video is in Trioz's current range
